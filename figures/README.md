# Figures

All figures are generated by the simulation scripts in `src/`. This directory
mirrors the figures presented in the paper.

---

## Main Figures (Paper Figures 1–4)

| File | Paper Figure | Description |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| `mean_payoff_comparison.png` | Figure 1 | Average payoff per round for NAFT vs TFT across noise levels. Shows crossover at high noise where TFT outperforms NAFT. |
| `cooperation_mass.png` | Figure 2 | Stationary probability of mutual cooperation (π_CC) vs noise level ε. NAFT sustains substantially higher cooperative mass. |
| `payoff_dominance.png` | Figure 3 | Payoff advantage Δ = Π(NAFT) − Π(TFT) vs noise. Positive values confirm NAFT dominance in low-to-moderate regimes. |
| `stationary_error.png` | Figure 4 | Max deviation between Monte Carlo estimates and closed-form stationary approximations. TFT error substantially exceeds NAFT error at high noise. |

## Key Findings Illustrated

- **Figures 1–2**: NAFT maintains ~0.9964 cooperative mass at ε=0.001 vs TFT's 0.7065; gap narrows but persists across the noise range.
- **Figure 3**: Payoff advantage peaks near ε=0.03 (Δ ≈ 0.89) and approaches zero around ε=0.20–0.25.
- **Figure 4**: NAFT's closed-form error remains below 0.21 across all noise levels; TFT's error exceeds 0.44 at high noise, reflecting DD-trap convergence.
- **Evolutionary figures**: NAFT achieves ~48% population share at low noise, displaced by Grim Trigger and AllD at ε ≥ 0.13.
